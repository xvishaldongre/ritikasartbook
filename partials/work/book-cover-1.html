The Moon's Lost Slipper

A bedtime cover about a moon who misplaces one shoe and borrows the tide to find it.

The brief called for ‘cosy, but with a tiny bit of adventure’. I kept the palette to moonlight lilac and cream so the title type can sit in the open sky, and gave the hills a gentle wobble so nothing feels ruled by a straight edge.
