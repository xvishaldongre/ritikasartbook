Pip and the Paper Whale

A small girl folds a whale from a bus ticket and sails it across the kitchen floor.

A cover that had to read at thumbnail size. One big shape (the whale), one warm face (Pip), and a lot of soft air around both. The peach wash was painted wet-on-wet so the paper grain stays visible in print.
