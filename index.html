💜 The artist

Soft pictures, made carefully

I’m Ritika — the artist behind ritikasartbook. I illustrate for children’s books and for the small, lovely things around them: covers, patterns, characters and the occasional editorial spread.

My work leans pastel and playful, with a strong line and a lot of heart. I like art that a child can read before they can read words — clear silhouettes, warm faces, and one feeling per picture.

Start a project 🚀

What I can make for you

  • 🎨Illustration for picture books, middle grade and editorial
  • 📚Book covers designed with space for type and shelf appeal
  • 🌸Seamless surface patterns for fabric, wallpaper and packaging
  • 🐾Character design with turnarounds, expressions and story bibles
  • 💬Friendly, deadline-respecting collaboration from sketch to print files

Studio replies usually within 1–2 working days.
